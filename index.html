WHY JARVIS EXISTS

JARVIS began as an experiment to move beyond the ordinary chatbot. The mission is to create a personal command layer that can understand intent, coordinate models, select tools and turn a request into an executable workflow.

Instead of treating AI as a single conversation window, JARVIS is designed as an ecosystem: reasoning, routing, automation, creation and feedback working together around the user.
